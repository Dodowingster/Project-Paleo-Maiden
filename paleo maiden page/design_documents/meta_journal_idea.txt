Core idea -> Since your paleo maiden's rivals do make up a big part of the game, what if we had 
a journal that logged all the details you currently know about each encountered rival for that 
specific run?

However, just like you, your rivals will also grow stronger and change their loadout as they go
through the motions. 

How this fits the core pillars:
- CHARACTERIZATION: allows us to better characterize enemies by playing to fill out the journal, allowing
us to better prepare for fighs against them while learning more about their preferences. This way, we can
justify more events with the enemies to learn more about them.
- COOL: see something cool the rivals do? you can document them in the journal too, showing you potential
synergies you can spec into.
- FGC: the journal acts similarly to how fighting game players take notes to prepare for tournaments, 
preparing for specific players that act as rivals in their stories.
- NURTURE: we can design the rivals to be brought up based on which techniques work well together too.
Players can use the knowledge from filling up the journal to learn ways to build their own maiden.

How it should work:
- everytime you meet a new potential rival, a new entry will appear in your journal
- the initial entry will contain the rival's current class. stats and techniques will be empty for now.
    - how will stats be filled?
        - events give you partial info on stats
        - fighting against them tells you all the stat values they currently have
    - how will techniques be filled?
        - events give you partial info on techniques
        - fighting against them tells you all the techniques they have in their loadout

How does the journal help you play?
- knowing color -> knowing strategy
- knowing stats -> knowing if you can beat someone at their own strategy
- knowing techniques -> can counterpick techniques to beat opponent
BUT:
- journal data is based on past fights, not the current fight
- opponent could have an adjusted loadout for the current fight, how can players prepare then?
    - THIS IS THE BIGGEST STOPPER METHINKS

How does this affect gameplay:
- Camp options:
    - Train -> -HP, +technique, +stat
    - Cook -> -ing, +HP, +aff
    - Rest -> +HP
    - if there's a rival at camp:
        - Spar -> -HP, +technique, +aff
        - naturally +journal, how much is filled is based on your relationship with the rival:
            - spar -> fully filled
            - camp event (randomly happens but does not matter what option you chose in camp):
                - before decision (battle or rest) -> partial fill
                - after decision -> partial fill if didnt battle
    - Takeaways:
        - at high HP, always train
        - at low HP, always cook/rest
        - when there's a rival:
            - at high HP:
                - default is fight
                - but if need stats then maybe better to train
            - at low HP:
                - training take less HP, can still consider training
                - more likely to rest/cook
- Map options:
    - Locals
        - one battle -> -HP, +technique, +aff
        - characters at locals, should they be considered rivals? if yes then journal is filled too
    - Arena
        - one battle -> -HP, +technique, +aff
        - get unique techniques
        - only available if you applied
    - Hazard
        - usually -HP or some debuff
    - Crossroads
        - watch other maidens spar -> +technique, +journal
    - Event
        - literally anything can happen lmao
    - Takeaways:
        - always avoid hazard if possible, unless going thru hazard helps you reach somewhere on time
            - to note, as long as on your path you pass thru a hazard during your turn, the hazard event will occur
        - empty mapnode is also avoided as much as possible, unless you can avoid a hazard
        - arena is always prioritized, so the plan is how to get strong enough in time for arena?
            - take more battles -> risk being downed and not being able to reach the arena in time
            - less battles -> not strong enough when you reach the arena?
            - routing: probably just head to the general arena area first and find as many battles around the area
            until arena day
                - when is this not smart: if the techniques you can earn around the arena aren't helpful for you to
                actually win the arena fight
                - but you are more likely to encounter the arena fight as a regular rival event, which lets you get
                info on their stats + loadout
        - locals or crossroads?
            - seems like always crossroads since you don't lose HP
            - but locals should have a larger technique pool (more colors to choose from)
                - but you have to get lucky with the color you want?
                - you go to locals most times anyways
            - now what if crossroads was a type of event, so you didnt know it was a crossroads?
            - now you're choosing between guaranteed power and non-guaranteed but more power?
