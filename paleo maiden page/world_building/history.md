## The Ring and the United Clades of Metazoa

The planet is called **Earth**. A faint ring of rocks circles it closer to the planet than Saturn's rings. From the ground, the ring appears small, and its fragments seem to float in the sky. They are not rocks, however, but enormous floating islands (also referred to as asteroids in early records): the home of the **United Clades of Metazoa**, a civilization representing the animal kingdom, including the Mammalias, Reptilias, and other clades.

The United Clades seek solid ground on Earth, targeting specifically the **Primeval Continents**, sacred to the Deinos. The Modern Continents lie outside the impact zone and would survive the initial catastrophe. To correct the timeline, the floating islands will join into a single, colossal mass and descend like a meteor, destroying the Primeval Continents and eradicating the Paleo Maidens. This invasion and impact are called **the Great Extinction**.

## The Origins of Sauris and Ornith, and the Arrival of Saurophaganax

In this world, Deinos is the ancient progenitor of two Paleo Maidens: Sauris and Ornith, who reside in the Primeval Continents. Sauris is lively and driven by raw passion, while Ornith is reserved and guided by keen insight. The two Maidens are constant rivals, disagreeing and fighting one another whenever their convictions collide.

Their disputes are said to cause natural disasters such as tsunamis, earthquakes, and tornadoes, for their power is immense. Every clash ends in a draw, with neither gaining victory over the other. Even so, the Maidens take a certain enjoyment in their battles.

Eventually, they tire of their unending clashes and agree to settle their dispute once and for all. They arrange a final bout in which they will fight with everything they have until neither can continue. During the battle, their opposing power tears reality apart, causing fossils and dinosaur skeletons to appear throughout the Primeval Continents.

Just as they prepare to release their ultimate attacks, they are interrupted by an outsider shaped like another child of Deinos who introduces himself as **Saurophaganax Maximus**. Unlike a normal Maiden, Saurophaganax is a living *nomen dubium*—an unresolvable anomaly born from clashing lineages and contradictions. Beneath his confrontation with the world lies a quiet envy of the Paleo Maiden world: because *Deinos* exists despite being an impossible anomaly in its own right, Saurophaganax longs for the validation and belonging among the Deinos that his own nature forbids.

Saurophaganax strikes Ornith and shatters her defenses, immediately drawing both Sauris and Ornith's attention to him as an outsider disrupting their duel. Revealing the looming threat of the Great Extinction as a countdown toward timeline correction, he forces Sauris and Ornith to stop fighting each other and act in total synchronization. Pouring their remaining life force into a **joint seal**—and sacrificing themselves in the process—they anchor the seal into a **massive stone mural wall** at the **southern edge of Laurasia** (at the **Chicxulub crater** site) that records the truth of the Great Extinction.

## Gideon's Discovery, the Mural Seal, and Owen's Preparations

Long before the Stratum Institute or the Great Expedition existed, **Dr. Gideon** discovered the progenitors' massive stone mural wall at the southern edge of Laurasia while researching on a Primeval Continent. Because the seal was manifested as a colossal mural anchored into the continent itself rather than a portable artifact, it remained in place. Gideon interpreted the mural as a warning of the Great Extinction: the children of Deinos were not meant to survive the coming correction of the timeline, and the United Clades of Metazoa were destined to rule Earth. The tablet also led him to believe that Paleo Maidens could face extinction within years of the Institute's founding.

Gideon shared his findings with **Dr. Owen**, founder of the PMU. Owen rejected the research publicly, fearing that disclosure would cause panic and provoke a preemptive war. Humans might fear that Maidens were connected to the apocalypse; Maidens might fight humanity over the Modern Continents as a refuge. Owen believed either conflict could fracture the cooperation needed to face the actual threat.

Privately, Owen treated Gideon's warning as credible enough to prepare for. With Owen's help, Gideon designed and built the **Stratum Institute** as a mobile base and potential ark: a vessel that could carry Maidens and staff, travel between regions, and perhaps escape the catastrophe. Its public rationale remained practical: mobility would improve access to the Primeval Continents and let more Maidens take part in the expedition.

Owen also shaped the **Great Expedition** and **World Cup League** as concealed military preparation. He presented them as an international athletic and academic program because openly training a Maiden-led military could provoke human fear and xenophobia. Assigning a human Lecturer or Professor to each Maiden was meant to build trust and teamwork across the two peoples. The faculty and Institute staff are unaware of Owen's full purpose; to them, the expedition and championships are genuine educational and sporting programs.

Over generations, each **World Cup League** cycle gradually weakens the ancient seal. Because Paleo Maidens inherit P-Magic from Sauris and Ornith, a Maiden whose P-Magic surges toward her ultimate potential can resonate with the seal and its seeping influence. Rather than being unsealed by a cult, Saurophaganax's influence leaks subtly through this resonance: while the player's chosen Maiden follows a path shaped by the Lecturer/Doctor's guidance and never encounters the mural, **core rivals** on separate expedition routes are drawn to the Chicxulub mural site in the modern timeline, causing Saurophaganax's seeping influence to pull them toward a darker, more volatile side of P-Magic.

## The Reveal, Time Loop Mechanics, and Endings

By the time of the current timeline's **World Cup League Grand Finals**, generations of World Cup cycles have worn the seal down to its weakest state, and the climax breaks the seal completely. At the end of the third year, after the World Cup Championship, the Maiden confronts **Saurophaganax Maximus**, who reveals the Great Extinction to both the Maiden and the Lecturer, claiming that it is necessary to restore the timeline and fulfill the United Clades' rightful dominion.

**How the time loop works:** In timelines where a core rival was drawn to the mural and fell under the strain of **Dark P-Magic**, that corruption pushes the rival to the brink of death after Saurophaganax's reveal—driving the player and Maiden to seal Saurophaganax to save the rival and avert the immediate Great Extinction. Sealing Saurophaganax draws the Dark P-Magic back into Saurophaganax and the Mural, causing the rival's own suppressed P-Magic to rebound and return to them. That sudden P-Magic rebound snaps the timeline backward to a checkpoint prior to the three-year expedition, saving the rival and postponing the Great Extinction.

**Memory wipe on loop:** Whenever a time loop occurs, everyone—including the Lecturer/player character—loses their memory of the cycle; it is as though nothing has happened yet.

In an individual loop reset, sealing Saurophaganax triggers this rebound rewind and wipes memories so the cycle can begin again, sparing the core rival and delaying the catastrophe without resolving the underlying threat.

The **True Ending** breaks that pattern through **parallel timeline convergence**. Across different parallel timelines, the roles shift—for instance, in one timeline the player guiding Stenops Stegosa confronts Saurophaganax while Fragilis Allosa falls under the strain of Dark P-Magic, whereas in another timeline the reverse occurs. Rather than erasing that corruption beforehand, convergence unites Maidens who have reached peak strength in their respective timelines so they can channel and donate their peak P-Magic to restore their fallen parallel counterparts, pulling them back from lethal Dark P-Magic strain and saving every core rival across the merged realities. With their counterparts restored and their combined strength at its height, the Maidens do not need to re-seal Saurophaganax to trigger a rewind—instead, they defeat Saurophaganax once and for all and allow history to move forward into the direct war against the approaching **United Clades of Metazoa** to defend Earth and the Primeval Continents.

## Inspirations and Direct Homage

In our real world, Dr. Gideon Mantell discovered the *Iguanodon* and shared his findings, but **Sir Richard Owen**—known for coining the word *"Dinosauria"* and founding major public institutions—was often dismissive and took credit for the work of his rivals.

This world-building reflects that historical dynamic: Dr. Owen is portrayed as a pragmatic, dismissive, and politically calculating figure who suppressed Dr. Gideon's research on The Great Extinction to prevent public panic. However, just like the real Richard Owen, Dr. Owen's public-facing legacy as the founder of the Stratum Institute and the master of the World Cup makes him far more famous and familiar to the public than Dr. Gideon, who passed away in obscurity. This tension between Gideon's empathetic vision and Owen's cold pragmatism drives the hidden conspiracies and historical foundations of *Project Paleo Maiden*.

The **Great Extinction** and the **Laurasia mural site** draw directly from the real-world **Chicxulub impact** at the end of the Cretaceous period (~66 million years ago), when a massive celestial asteroid struck the Yucatán Peninsula and triggered the K–Pg extinction event that wiped out non-avian dinosaurs. In *Project Paleo Maiden*, this historical cataclysm is literalized and localized: Sauris and Ornith anchored their joint seal and warning mural at the southern edge of Laurasia (the equivalent of the Chicxulub crater), while the descending floating islands of the United Clades of Metazoa reenact that meteoric reckoning.

The United Clades of Metazoa is inspired by the Animal Kingdom. The timeline's intended course follows the history familiar to us: the dinosaurs become extinct, and the Mammals and Reptiles flourish in the world that follows. The Paleo Maidens, as living manifestations of the Deinos, are an anomaly in this world: they survive into an age in which the Deinos were meant to be gone. Their continued existence prevents the United Clades from fully manifesting and claiming Earth as their own.
